Situation

Action

MEW 132kV Substation Upgrade

Situation
Aging 132kV/11kV substations in the Ahmadi governorate were operating beyond rated capacity, risking grid instability during peak summer loads.
Action
Led the complete redesign of protection relay coordination and switchgear replacement. Performed load flow and short-circuit analysis using ETAP to validate the new configuration.
Result
Increased substation capacity by 35% and eliminated critical fault risks. Zero safety incidents during the 14-month commissioning period.
35% Capacity Increase | Zero Incidents | 14-Month Delivery

Predictive Maintenance Program

Situation
KOC's rotating equipment fleet experienced frequent unplanned failures, costing an estimated KWD 500K annually in lost production and emergency repairs.
Action
Implemented a condition-based maintenance program using vibration analysis sensors, thermal imaging, and Python-based anomaly detection on critical rotating equipment.
Result
Reduced unplanned downtime by 28% in the first year. Program was adopted as a KOC best practice and expanded to 3 additional facilities.
28% Downtime Reduction | KWD 500K Saved | 3 Facilities Expanded
