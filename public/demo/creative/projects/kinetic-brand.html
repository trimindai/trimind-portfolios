Brand & Motion

Kinetic Brand

Client
On Running
Year
2024
Role
Art Director & Motion Lead
Duration
8 weeks

A comprehensive brand motion system for On Running's 2024 product launch campaign. The project merged kinetic typography with fluid 3D elements to communicate the brand's core philosophy of movement and lightness.

Working closely with On Running's in-house team, we developed a modular motion language that scales across social media, retail displays, and digital experiences. Every animation references the biomechanics of running — from the cushioned impact to the elastic push-off.

The deliverables included a 60-second hero film, a library of 24 social assets, in-store motion graphics for flagship locations, and a comprehensive motion guidelines document for internal teams.
