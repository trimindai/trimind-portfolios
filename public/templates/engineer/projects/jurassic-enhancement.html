Jurassic Production Enhancement Program

$420M capital program delivering 35,000 bopd production uplift through 12 horizontal wells with intelligent completions across North Kuwait’s Jurassic reservoirs.

Kuwait’s Jurassic reservoirs in the northern fields hold significant untapped potential, but the carbonate formations present complex drilling and completion challenges. This program was designed to unlock incremental production through modern horizontal well technology, targeting the Marrat, Minjur, and Dharuma formations.

As lead engineer, I was responsible for the full lifecycle: concept screening, well trajectory design, completion architecture, surface facility tie-ins, and production ramp-up. The program delivered ahead of the 2025 target and under the original budget estimate.

Challenge

The Jurassic carbonates in Sabriyah have highly heterogeneous permeability, with production dominated by natural fracture networks rather than matrix flow. Conventional vertical wells were producing below 1,500 bopd each, and water coning was reducing well life to under 5 years.

Approach

We designed long-reach horizontal wells (2,800–4,600m lateral length) targeting fracture corridors identified through seismic attribute analysis and image log interpretation. Each well was equipped with 4–6 ICV zones, allowing real-time production optimization and water shut-off without workover intervention.

Result

Average well IP of 3,800 bopd—more than 2.5x the vertical well benchmark. Water cut managed below 15% across the program through proactive ICV management. Unit development cost of $12/bbl, 30% below the previous Jurassic campaign.
