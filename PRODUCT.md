# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kuwait and GCC professionals: engineers, corporate executives, designers, and technical specialists building career portfolios. They're typically mid-career, bilingual (Arabic/English), and need something that looks premium without spending hours on design. Context: sitting at a desk after work, or on a phone during lunch, filling in career details they already know. The job is "make me look as good on screen as I am in person."

## Product Purpose

Portfolio Pro lets GCC professionals build a polished, hosted portfolio in minutes for a one-time payment (1.5 KWD). No subscriptions, no design skills needed. Success looks like: a professional opens the app, picks a template, fills in their info, pays once, and walks away with a live URL and print-ready PDF they're genuinely proud to share on LinkedIn or hand to a recruiter.

## Brand Personality

Expert, polished, confident. The voice says "we've already made the design decisions for you" without being condescending. Three words: **assured, refined, effortless**. The emotional goal is quiet confidence: the user should feel like they hired a designer, not like they used a template builder.

## Anti-references

- **AI-generated aesthetic.** Gradient blobs, glassmorphism cards, big-number hero metrics with gradient accents, identical card grids with icons. The "every AI startup landing page" look. Portfolio Pro should feel hand-crafted, not generated.
- **Cheap page builders.** Wix free-tier sites, generic WordPress themes, Canva website builder. Cluttered sidebars, clip-art icons, inconsistent spacing, "built with" badges. Portfolio Pro is the antidote to these.
- **Generic SaaS cream.** Warm beige backgrounds, soft illustrations, rounded-everything. Portfolio Pro is for professionals building career presence, not a lifestyle app.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the output, not the tool.** The portfolio templates are the hero. Every screen should remind the user what they're building, not how the builder works.
2. **Bilingual is native, not bolted on.** Arabic RTL is a first-class layout, not a mirrored afterthought. Typography, spacing, and hierarchy must feel intentional in both directions.
3. **One path, no dead ends.** The flow is linear: pick template, fill info, preview, pay, publish. Minimize branches, eliminate confusion. Every screen has one obvious next action.
4. **Earn trust through craft.** GCC professionals are paying for quality. Every detail (transitions, alignment, copy) signals that this product is worth the price.
5. **Templates are the brand.** The quality of the generated portfolios IS the product's reputation. A mediocre template destroys trust faster than a buggy dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance as baseline: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components, full keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text, screen reader support. Arabic RTL is a first-class priority: layout, reading order, number formatting, and typographic conventions must be native-quality, not CSS-mirrored shortcuts. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`. Support for color blindness in any data visualization or status indicators (never rely on color alone).
