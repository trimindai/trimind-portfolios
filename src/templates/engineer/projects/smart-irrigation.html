Smart Irrigation System

A camera, a soil sensor, and a CNN — plants get the right amount of water without human guesswork.

Plants in Kuwait are watered on guesswork or fixed timers — wasting water and damaging crops. We built a system that sees the plant, reads the soil, and only waters when both agree.

Plant detection
≥90% accuracy (CNN, 3 species)
Response time
<3s from dry soil to pump activation
Total cost
55 KWD (21% below commercial alternatives)
Challenge

Commercial controllers (Hunter X2-401, ~70 KWD) water on a timer — they can't sense soil moisture or identify plants.

Solution

Dual-microcontroller design: Raspberry Pi runs a CNN over a camera to identify the species, Arduino reads soil moisture and drives the pump. Water only flows when both agree.
